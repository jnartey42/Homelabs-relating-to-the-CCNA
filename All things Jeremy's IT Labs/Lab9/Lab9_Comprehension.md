Jeremy's IT Labs on YouTube

Comprehension: The lab and video references Jeremy's Day 9 (Switch Interfaces) video pertaining to the CCNA. Jeremy discusses how interfaces on a switch works (layer 1) of the TCP layer model. He discusses what's different between interfaces on a switch, in comparison to a router which was discussed earlier in his series. Switches often have more interfaces to work with. He goes through various commands that one can use on the Cisco CLI, such as `show ip interfaces brief`, which shows a table featuring information such as the interface, IP address (even though its a switch, they will all remain unassigned because it works at Layer 2 of the TCP model), and configuration status of whether its operational, and its status. Switch interfaces don't have the shutdown command enabled by default. Jeremy talks about the importance of full and half duplex operation (whether devices are allowed to process and send data at the same time, or do each operation one by one), why CSMA/CD (Carrier Sense Multiple Access with Collision Detection) is important, how interfaces negotiate internet speeds, and possible errors that can occur as an interface tries to send out data.
